    Where the numbers come from

    No single leaderboard measures everything people care about, so each requirement is scored from the benchmarks that best measure it. Those measurements are turned into a 0–10 rating by hand, and that step is a judgment call. The ratings show how the models compare with each other, not how good they are in absolute terms.

    Your fit score is a weighted average: each model's rating on a requirement, times how much you care about it, added up and divided by the total of your weights, then scaled to 100.
